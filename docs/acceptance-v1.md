- Wi-Fi-off proof: every test ran with `TIDE_OFFLINE_GUARD=1`.

Not covered by anything on this machine (all 🟡): the Mac-only items (dmg + Open Anyway, window-closed playback, media keys) and everything on her iPhone (MP3, m4b in Apple Books).
