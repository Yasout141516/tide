2.3 Memory and Attention

Working memory holds a small amount of information for a short time, and it is easily disrupted. Most adults can keep about four items in mind at once. Attention decides which of those items stay active and which quietly fade. This is why reading while distracted feels like nothing sticks, and why short, focused sessions work better than long, scattered ones.
